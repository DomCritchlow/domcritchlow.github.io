About

Dominic Critchlow is a forward-deployed product manager at Ode with Anthropic in New York, where he builds and deploys AI systems with enterprise teams.

Previously, Dominic spent nearly a decade in national security. At the Department of Defense’s Chief Digital and Artificial Intelligence Office, he co-founded Gamechanger, an AI-powered policy search platform. He built the first version himself; it grew to 1,500 users and a 25-person team, indexed millions of documents across the department, and was recognized by the National Security Commission on AI.

He later served as Chief Scientist for Booz Allen Hamilton in Europe, leading a 60-person engineering organization that built AI-enabled decision-support software for U.S. European Command and U.S. Africa Command.

Dominic teaches graduate Big Data Analytics at Seton Hall University’s Stillman School of Business and serves as a Military Intelligence officer in the U.S. Army Reserve. He studied physics and computer science at Austin Peay State University.

On his own time he builds small things: two iOS apps on the App Store, Pinned and Helio; Flags, which tracks how often every country is mentioned in U.S. congressional records; and radio and machine learning experiments.

The best way to reach Dominic is LinkedIn. His code is on GitHub.
