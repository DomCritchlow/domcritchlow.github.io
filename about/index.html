About

Dominic Critchlow is an AI practitioner and technology leader focused on how organizations build and deploy AI systems. He works in forward-deployed AI in New York, helping enterprise teams turn new model capabilities into production software.

Previously, Dominic spent nearly a decade in national security. At the U.S. Department of Defense’s Chief Digital and Artificial Intelligence Office, he co-founded Gamechanger, an AI-powered policy search platform that grew from an early product into a 25-person team and an enterprise capability indexing millions of documents across the Department of Defense.

He later served as Chief Scientist for Booz Allen Hamilton in Europe, leading a 60-person engineering organization building AI-enabled software and decision-support capabilities for U.S. European Command and U.S. Africa Command.

His broader interests include AI-enabled institutions, government software, human-computer collaboration, and how organizations turn emerging technology into durable operational capability. He writes and speaks about AI adoption and software delivery in government.

Dominic also teaches graduate Big Data Analytics at Seton Hall University’s Stillman School of Business and serves as a Military Intelligence officer in the U.S. Army Reserve. He maintains an active technical practice through independent projects spanning machine learning, AI automation, data visualization, native applications, and radio systems. He studied physics and computer science at Austin Peay State University and earned a master’s degree in computer science from George Mason University.

Outside of work I like to experiment: iOS apps, data visualizations, and radio + machine learning projects that start as a simple question and turn into something useful, or at least fun. A recent one is Flags, which tracks how often every country is mentioned across bills, nominations, amendments, and the Congressional Record.

The best way to reach me is LinkedIn. You can also find me on GitHub.
