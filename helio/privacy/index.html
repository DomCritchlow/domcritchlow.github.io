Privacy Policy

Helio · Last updated July 5, 2026

Overview

Helio is a space weather app: it shows what the sun is doing and what that means for your sky. Your data belongs to you and stays on your device.

Data We Collect

We collect no data. Helio has no servers and sends no information to the developer or to any analytics, advertising, or tracking service.

To show current conditions, the app fetches public data feeds directly from NOAA (services.swpc.noaa.gov) and NASA (sdo.gsfc.nasa.gov for solar imagery, kauai.ccmc.gsfc.nasa.gov for CME forecasts). These requests contain no account, identifier, or location information; they are the same requests a web browser would make to those public sites.

Location

Helio can use your device's location, only while the app is open and only if you allow it, to localize the aurora outlook: your local darkness window, how far north or south you are, and where you sit on the aurora map. This is computed on your device.

  • Your location is never sent to the developer and never included in any data feed request.
  • To name your place (for example, "north of Portland"), the app uses Apple's MapKit reverse geocoding, which is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; the developer never receives it.
  • Location is entirely optional. If you deny the prompt, every feature keeps working with a planetary view.

Data Stored on Your Device

Helio caches the most recent data feeds and solar image on your device so the app can open instantly and work offline. This cache never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app. Helio stores no personal information.

Third-Party SDKs and Analytics

Helio uses no third-party SDKs, analytics frameworks, advertising networks, or crash-reporting services.

Children's Privacy

Helio does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. (It collects no information from anyone.)

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the app will be updated with a new "Last updated" date.
