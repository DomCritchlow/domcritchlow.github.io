I'm Dominic Critchlow. I build and deploy AI products, currently as a forward-deployed product manager at Ode with Anthropic. Before that I led AI engineering teams for the Department of Defense. On the side I teach a graduate Big Data Analytics course at Seton Hall.

Off hours I make small things for the fun of it: iOS apps, data visualizations, and radio + machine learning experiments. The latest is Pinned, a native iOS app for saving the places you care about, now on the App Store. I'm also having a lot of fun with Flags, which tracks how often every country comes up in U.S. congressional language.

  • Pinned
    project · June 13, 2026

    A native iOS app for saving the places you care about. Drop a pin, scan a storefront, or save from a shared link — then organize everything into Collections, filter by tag, and navigate back when you're ready. Built with SwiftUI, SwiftData, MapKit, and private CloudKit sync. No servers, no tracking.

    project · github · April 23, 2026
