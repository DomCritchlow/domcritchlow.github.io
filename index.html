I'm Dominic Critchlow, a forward-deployed product manager at Ode with Anthropic in New York.

Before Ode I spent nearly a decade in national security. I co-founded Gamechanger at the Department of Defense's Chief Digital and AI Office, then served as Chief Scientist for Booz Allen Hamilton in Europe.

I teach graduate analytics at Seton Hall and serve in the U.S. Army Reserve. On my own time I make iOS apps like Pinned and Helio, and data projects like Flags.

Latest

  • Helio
    project · July 05, 2026

    A native iOS weather app for the sun, the Earth, and the moon. Live NASA and NOAA imagery, aurora forecasts for your sky, and a system map of the spacecraft feeding it. No servers, no tracking.

  • Pinned
    project · June 13, 2026

    A native iOS app for saving places. Save from a link, a screenshot, or your camera, organize into Collections, and navigate back later. SwiftUI, SwiftData, MapKit, and private CloudKit sync. No servers, no tracking.

  • Helio (web)
    project · github · March 05, 2026

    Solar activity visualizer on live NOAA data. The sun renders as a pixel grid with sunspot regions at their real heliographic coordinates, and a 28-day scrubber replays the past month.

  • Flags
    project · github · February 28, 2026

    Tracks how often every country is mentioned across U.S. bills, nominations, amendments, and the Congressional Record.

  • Vibecast
    project · github · December 14, 2025

    A daily podcast generated end to end: scheduled ingestion, script writing, text-to-speech, and an RSS feed. Runs unattended on GitHub Actions.

  • ForkFeed
    project · github · December 13, 2025

    A self-hosted microblog with RSS aggregation. Post short updates and photos, follow any feed, and read everything in one timeline. Runs on GitHub Pages with no server or database.

  • Latitude
    project · github · December 09, 2025

    Draws your line of latitude around the Earth, with live temperatures at every point along it. Geolocation, horizontal panning, and shareable URLs.

  • airwaveml
    project · github · December 09, 2025

    Deep-learning decoder for radio and audio signals in PyTorch. Reproducible training (CTC and attention variants), dataset tooling, and experiments toward small, accurate models.
