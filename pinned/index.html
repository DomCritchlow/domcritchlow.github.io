📌 Pinned

Save the places you love. Drop a pin, scan a storefront, or save from a shared link — then find your way back when you're ready.

Coming soon to the App Store.

What it's for

  • I'm standing in front of something I want to save. Drop a pin or scan the sign with your camera.
  • I heard about a place and want to find it fast. Search, or save it straight from a shared link.
  • What should I explore around me right now? See what's nearby on the map.
  • Help me remember, revisit, and navigate. Notes, visits, and one-tap directions.

Features

  • Collections — group places into lists, with smart rules that file new pins automatically
  • Tags & filters — narrow the map to just what you're looking for
  • Scan to save — point your camera at a storefront or sign to identify a place
  • Save from anywhere — a share extension pulls places in from Maps links and the web
  • Private by design — your data lives on your devices and syncs through your own iCloud. No servers, no analytics, no tracking.

Privacy

Pinned collects nothing. Everything you save stays on your device and syncs privately through your personal iCloud account. Read the full privacy policy.

Built with SwiftUI, SwiftData, MapKit, and CloudKit · by Dominic Critchlow
