Pinned

Save the places you love — from a link, a screenshot, a map, or your camera. Pinned works out the real spot, right on your device, then helps you organize and find your way back.

Download on the App Store

Free · iPhone & iPad

What it's for

  • I'm standing in front of something I want to save. Drop a pin or point your camera at the sign.
  • A friend sent me a place. Share the link, screenshot, or address to Pinned — it works out the real spot for you.
  • I want to plan a day out. Build a Plan: ordered stops with times, distances, and ETAs.
  • Help me remember, revisit, and navigate. Notes, visits, and one-tap directions.

Features

  • Place Intelligence — save from a link, screenshot, address, or your camera, and Pinned resolves it to the real place, right on your device
  • Collections — group places into lists, with smart rules that file new pins automatically
  • Plans — build a day out as ordered stops with times, distances, and directions
  • Tags & filters — narrow the map to just what you're looking for
  • Across iPhone & iPad — Siri, Shortcuts, Spotlight, Visual Intelligence, and shareable pin links
  • Private by design — your data lives on your devices and syncs through your own iCloud. No servers, no analytics, no tracking.

Privacy

Pinned collects nothing. Everything you save stays on your device and syncs privately through your personal iCloud account. Read the full privacy policy.

Built with SwiftUI, SwiftData, MapKit, and CloudKit · by Dominic Critchlow
