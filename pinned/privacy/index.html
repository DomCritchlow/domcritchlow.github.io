Privacy Policy

Pinned · Last updated June 13, 2026

Overview

Pinned is a personal place-saving app. Your data belongs to you and stays on your devices. We have no servers and collect nothing about you.

Data We Collect

We collect no data. Pinned has no servers and sends no information to the developer or to any analytics, advertising, or tracking service.

To provide its core features, the app uses Apple's MapKit — searching for places, autocompleting names, and generating directions send your search terms and, for directions, the relevant coordinates to Apple so it can return results. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; the developer never receives this data. When you save a place from a shared link, the app may contact that link's host (for example, a Maps short link) to resolve it to a real address.

Data Stored on Your Devices

Pinned stores the following data locally on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, synced privately through your personal iCloud account (Apple CloudKit):

  • Places — names, coordinates, notes, tags, and hero images you save
  • Collections — lists you create to organize your places
  • App preferences — map style, sort order, distance units, and other settings

This data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple's iCloud infrastructure. The developer has no access to it.

Location

Pinned requests access to your device's location only while the app is open, to show your position on the map and help you find and save nearby places. The developer never stores, syncs, or receives your location. When you search for nearby places, the relevant location is sent to Apple's MapKit to return results, as described above.

Camera

Pinned can use your camera so you can scan a sign or storefront to identify and save a place. Camera access is requested only when you start a scan, and images are processed on your device. Photos captured this way are not uploaded to any server and are not sent to the developer.

Hero Images

Hero images (Look Around snapshots and map previews) are generated automatically by Apple's MapKit APIs and cached locally on your device. They are not uploaded to any server. If you delete a saved place, its cached image is deleted from your device.

Third-Party SDKs and Analytics

Pinned uses no third-party SDKs, analytics frameworks, advertising networks, or crash-reporting services.

Children's Privacy

Pinned does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the app will be updated with a new "Last updated" date.
